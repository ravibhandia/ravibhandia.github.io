Every night

8-minute left hip routine

  1. TFL / upper-quad ball release · 1–2 min. Stay on the muscle, not the joint.
  2. Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch with TFL bias · 2 × 30–45 sec.
  3. Prone internal-rotation drops · 1 × 8 easy reps.
  4. 90/90 left-leg-rear sink · 45–60 sec, controlled and pain-free.
  5. Seated IR windshield wipers · 10 total reps.

Primary goal

Mobility overview

Current contract-relax rule: move under control to the edge of the stretch. Contract out against the position for 3–5 sec, then contract in toward deeper active range for 3–5 sec. Normal effort is 7–9/10. The left hamstring remains capped at 5–6/10 until symptom-stable and deliberately reviewed.

Muscle pull is acceptable; joint pinch is a stop signal. No bouncing. Progress contraction quality first, then reps, then depth or load. Mobility work happens after lifting or standalone, never before heavy lifts.

About 20 min

Handstands before planche

1. Handstand · 5–8 min

  • All kick-ups use the right leg only.
  • 2–3 wall kick-ups, 2–3 chest-to-wall holds of 10–20 sec, then 3–5 crisp freestanding attempts.
  • Push tall, ribs down, glutes tight, use fingertip pressure, and stop before shaking.
  • Friday: 2–3 attempts total to protect military press performance.

2. Parallette planche · about 12 min

  • Slow planche walks · 2–3 passes.
  • Planche leans · 2–3 × 10–15 sec, never to failure.
  • Band-assisted tuck planche · 2–3 × 10–15 sec.
  • If wrists ache, cut walk passes first. If the elbow reacts, cut lean duration first.

Block D cut rule: when the skill block expands the session, finishers are the first and official cut. Do not steal time from the heavy main lift, interleaved abs, or mobility and rehab blocks.

Weekday target · about 90 min

Lifting split

Session map: 0:00–0:10 warm-up · 0:10–0:25 skill block · 0:25–0:45 Block A · 0:45–1:05 Block B · 1:05–1:20 Block C · final 10 min for Block D or scheduled mobility.

How the blocks and ab rounds work

  • Run each block as a superset or tri-set: move from A1 to A2 (or B1 to B2) before resting.
  • Block A pairs the heavy main lift only with a low-fatigue skill drill. Rest 75–90 sec after the full pairing.
  • Blocks B and C use non-competing movements. Rest 45–60 sec only after the complete pairing.
  • Tuesday and Thursday: complete Abs Round 1 after Block A, Round 2 after Block B, and Round 3 after Block C.
  • Block D is optional and always gets cut first when time is short.

Monday · Legs

Block A · 3 working rounds

  1. A1Hack Squat Machine · 50–60% effort, strict 3-1-1 tempo. Compare left/right drive honestly.
  2. A2Press-to-handstand drill · choose a band-assisted straddle press hold for 10–15 sec or parallette pike compression lifts for 8–10 reps, ≤6/10 effort.

Block B · 3 rounds

  1. B1Bulgarian split squat · bilateral full ROM, 8–10 per leg; watch left asymmetry.
  2. B2Low-fatigue core option · use only if it does not duplicate the main ab circuit.

Block C · 3 rounds

  1. C1Seated hamstring curl · 12 reps with a slow eccentric.
  2. C2Single-leg hip thrust · 10–12 per leg with control.

Optional Block D: calf and tibialis work as time allows. Pick only one loaded hinge because Mobility A already supplies hamstring exposure.

Tuesday · Chest / triceps

Block A · 3–4 rounds

  1. A1Barbell bench press · 6–8 at RPE 7–8.
  2. A2Feet-elevated pike push-ups · 5–8 at ≤6/10 effort, 2 sec down + 1 sec pause. Keep the elbows fixed in one tracking path over the wrists; they bend and straighten without flaring outward.

Then Abs circuit · Round 1 of 3

Block B · 3 rounds

  1. B1Incline DB press · 8–10 with a 2-sec descent.
  2. B2Rope overhead extension or pressdown · 10–12; no skull crushers.

Then Abs circuit · Round 2 of 3

Block C · 3 rounds

  1. C1Low-to-high cable fly · 12–15 at 3-1-1.
  2. C2Rope overhead triceps extension · 12–15; use a pressdown if the elbows are cranky.

Then Abs circuit · Round 3 of 3

Optional Block D: bodyweight dips + rope pressdowns for 2–3 rounds. Cut this block first.

Thursday · Back

Fresh primer: optional muscle-ups for 5–8 min before Block A.

Block A · 3–4 rounds

  1. A1Weighted or neutral-grip pull-ups · 4–6 at RPE 7.
  2. A2Slider pike pulls · 4–6 slow reps at ≤6/10 effort.

Then Abs circuit · Round 1 of 3

Block B · 3 rounds

  1. B1Lat pulldown · 8–12 with a full stretch at the top.
  2. B2Seated cable row · 10–12 with a hard 1-sec squeeze. Rotate in chest-supported DB rows week to week rather than stacking both rows.

Do not superset the pulldown and row back-to-back; separate the pulling slots with recovery so performance stays crisp.

Then Abs circuit · Round 2 of 3

Block C · 3 rounds

  1. C1Straight-arm cable press-down · 12 controlled reps.
  2. C2Seated pike compression lifts · 8–10 strict reps; keep for pancake and press carryover.

Then Abs circuit · Round 3 of 3

Optional Block D: cable pull-through or 45° back extension. Skip it if the back is irritated or the week already includes heavy hinging.

Friday · Shoulders

Block A · 3–4 rounds

  1. A1Standing military press · 5–6 at RPE 6–7. During a hip flare, use a seated back-supported DB overhead press or seated Arnold press.
  2. A2Feet-elevated pike push-ups · 5–8 at ≤6/10 effort. Keep the elbows fixed in one tracking path over the wrists; they bend and straighten without flaring outward.

Block B · 3 rounds

  1. B1Cable lateral raise · 10–12/arm.
  2. B2Reverse fly · 12–15 with a 1-sec squeeze.

Block C · 3 rounds

  1. C1Straight-arm cable press-down · 12 controlled reps.
  2. C2Half-kneeling landmine press · 8–10 per side only when the position is comfortable; otherwise use an Arnold press.
  3. C3Seated pike compression lifts · 8–10 strict reps.

Optional Block D: DB shrugs + face pulls for 2–3 rounds. Cut this block first.

Tuesday · Thursday · Saturday

Abs hypertrophy circuit

Complete three rounds. On Tuesday and Thursday, place one round after each of Blocks A, B, and C. Saturday is standalone with 60–90 sec between rounds.

  1. Weighted knee crunches · primary progressive-overload movement.
  2. Leg lifts · if the front left hip pinches, use bent-knee reverse crunches.
  3. Single-leg marches · same hip rule; replace with bent-knee reverse crunches when needed.
  4. Around the world · 8 reps.
  5. Crunches · 4 each left, middle, and right.
  6. Tuck hold · controlled technical failure.

Keep seated pike compression lifts 1 × 8–10 twice weekly for pancake and press carryover. Do not stack the old embedded core blocks on top of this circuit.

Cardio

Cycling and sprint return

  • Target two rides per week minimum: Tuesday or Thursday evening plus a weekend ride.
  • Until the hamstring is fully symptom-stable, avoid out-of-saddle sprints and hard standing climbs.
  • Sunday evening is Zone 2 only because Monday is leg day.
  • Wednesday remains sprint warm-up drills only until cleared to progress; return through A-skips, B-skips, then 60–70% strides.
  • Cycling is additive cardio and does not replace leg day or the planned hamstring exposures.
