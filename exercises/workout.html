Ravi’s Training Plan

Flexibility-first training with hypertrophy lifting, handstand and planche skill work, cycling, and a conservative return from left hamstring and anterior-hip irritation.

Guardrails Week Night rehab Flexibility Skills Lifting Core Progress

Non-negotiables right now

  • No cartwheels. Every handstand kick-up starts from the right leg.
  • Left hip internal rotation: gentle 3–4/10 effort only; pain must stay at or below 2/10.
  • Left hamstring: end-range contractions stay at 5–6/10 through approximately Aug 9; left-forward slider negatives wait until Aug 16 or later.
  • Elbow: pain at or below 2/10 with no next-morning stiffness. Skull crushers remain banned.
  • If time runs short: cut Block D first. Protect the main sets, interleaved abs, skill work, and scheduled flexibility/rehab.

Status

Current constraints

Left anterior hip / TFL

Upper rectus femoris/TFL irritation followed bent-left-leg cartwheel kick-ups. Avoid the trigger, keep left-leg hip airplanes shallow, and use seated back-supported pressing during flare-ups.

Left hamstring

Grade 1 strain from approximately July 12. No sprinting until the return sequence is symptom-stable: drills, then 60–70% strides, then gradual speed exposure.

Left triceps tendon

Continue progressive loading. If planche leans irritate it, reduce lean duration before reducing frequency. Stretching and massage are comfort tools, not the rehab stimulus.

Primary goals

Pancake, front split, and active 90/90 rotation; abs hypertrophy three times weekly; handstand push-up progress; planche progress; and maintenance of the hypertrophy split.

Every night

8-minute left hip routine

  1. TFL / upper-quad ball release · 1–2 minutes. Stay on the muscle, not the joint.
  2. Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch with TFL bias · 2 × 30–45 seconds.
  3. Prone internal-rotation drops · 1 × 8 easy reps.
  4. 90/90 left-leg-rear sink · 45–60 seconds, controlled and pain-free.
  5. Seated IR windshield wipers · 10 total reps.

Primary goal

Flexibility method and sessions

Current contract-relax rule: move under control to the edge of the stretch. Contract out against the position for 3–5 seconds, then contract in toward deeper active range for 3–5 seconds. Normal effort is 7–9/10. The left hamstring remains capped at 5–6/10 until symptom-stable after Aug 9.

Muscle pull is acceptable; joint pinch is a stop signal. No bouncing. Progress contraction quality first, then reps, then depth or load. Flexibility work happens after lifting or standalone, never before heavy lifts.

Session A Pancake + adductors

Friday · about 12–15 minutes

  1. Seated good mornings on a bench · 2 × 8, light load at chest.
  2. Weighted pancake pulls · 2 × 6–8, chest leads and spine stays long.
  3. Loaded Cossack squats · 2 × 6/side; keep the left-bent side shallow.
  4. Copenhagen plank · 2 × 15–20 sec/side.

Session B Hamstring + splits

Wednesday · doubles as hamstring rehab

  1. Elevated pike hinges · 2 × 8–10.
  2. Single-leg RDL · 2 × 8/side; left side shallower.
  3. Elephant walks · 1 × 10/side, gentle left lockout.
  4. Half-split slider negatives · right leg forward only; left waits until Aug 16+.
  5. Couch-stretch lift-offs · 2 × 6 controlled reps/side.

Session C 90/90 + hip control

Monday after legs · left IR stays 3–4/10

  1. 90/90 switches · 1 × 10 total.
  2. 90/90 forward lean contract-relax · 1 × 6/side.
  3. 90/90 lift-offs · front-shin ER and rear-foot IR, 2 × 5 each.
  4. Prone IR drops · 1 × 8 with a 4-second wall-press contraction.
  5. Hand-supported hip airplanes · 2 × 5/side; shallow hinge on left standing leg.

About 20 minutes

Handstands before planche

1. Handstand · 5–8 minutes

  • All kick-ups use the right leg only.
  • 2–3 wall kick-ups, 2–3 chest-to-wall holds of 10–20 sec, then 3–5 crisp freestanding attempts.
  • Push tall, ribs down, glutes tight, use fingertip pressure, and stop before shaking.
  • Friday: 2–3 attempts total to protect military press performance.

2. Parallette planche · about 12 minutes

  • Slow planche walks · 2–3 passes.
  • Planche leans · 2–3 × 10–15 sec, never to failure.
  • Band-assisted tuck planche · 2–3 × 10–15 sec.
  • If wrists ache, cut walk passes first. If the elbow reacts, cut lean duration first.

Block D cut rule: when the skill block expands the session, finishers are the first and official cut. Do not steal time from the heavy main lift, interleaved abs, or flexibility and rehab blocks.

Weekday target · about 90 minutes

Lifting split

Session map: 0:00–0:10 warm-up · 0:10–0:30 skill block · 0:30–0:50 Block A · 0:50–1:10 Block B · 1:10–1:25 Block C · final minutes for Block D or scheduled flexibility. Pair heavy work only with low-fatigue skill or core; Blocks B onward use non-competing supersets.

Monday · Legs

  • Hack Squat Machine · 3 working sets at 50–60% effort, strict 3-1-1 tempo. Compare left/right drive honestly.
  • Bulgarian split squat · bilateral full ROM, 8–10/leg; watch left asymmetry.
  • Seated hamstring curl · 3 × 12–15, light with slow eccentric.
  • Single-leg hip thrust · 10–12/leg; calf and tibialis work as time allows.
  • Pick only one loaded hinge because Flex B already supplies hamstring exposure.

Tuesday · Chest / triceps

  • Barbell bench · 3–4 × 6–8 at RPE 7–8.
  • Incline DB press · 3 × 8–10.
  • Low-to-high cable fly · 3 × 12–15.
  • Rope overhead extension or pressdown · 10–15; no skull crushers.
  • Dips and pressdowns are Block D and get cut first.

Thursday · Back

  • Optional fresh muscle-up primer · 5–8 min.
  • Weighted or neutral-grip pull-ups · 3–4 × 4–6 at RPE 7.
  • Lat pulldown · 8–12 and seated cable row · 10–12, paired with abs rather than each other.
  • Straight-arm cable press-down · 12; seated pike compression · 8–10.
  • Pull-through or back extension is optional Block D.

Friday · Shoulders

  • Standing military press · 3–4 × 5–6 at RPE 6–7.
  • During a hip flare: use seated back-supported DB overhead press or seated Arnold press.
  • Cable lateral raise · 10–12 and reverse fly · 12–15.
  • Landmine press · 8–10/side only when the half-kneeling position is comfortable.
  • Shrugs and face pulls are optional Block D.

Tuesday · Thursday · Saturday

Abs hypertrophy circuit

Complete three rounds. On Tuesday and Thursday, place one round after each of Blocks A, B, and C. Saturday is standalone with 60–90 seconds between rounds.

  1. Weighted knee crunches · primary progressive-overload movement.
  2. Leg lifts · if the front left hip pinches, use bent-knee reverse crunches.
  3. Single-leg marches · same hip rule; replace with bent-knee reverse crunches when needed.
  4. Around the world · 8 reps.
  5. Crunches · 4 each left, middle, and right.
  6. Tuck hold · controlled technical failure.

Keep seated pike compression lifts 1 × 8–10 twice weekly for pancake and press carryover. Do not stack the old embedded core blocks on top of this circuit.

Cardio

Cycling and sprint return

  • Target two rides per week minimum: Tuesday or Thursday evening plus a weekend ride.
  • Until the hamstring is fully symptom-stable, avoid out-of-saddle sprints and hard standing climbs.
  • Sunday evening is Zone 2 only because Monday is leg day.
  • Wednesday remains sprint warm-up drills only until cleared to progress; return through A-skips, B-skips, then 60–70% strides.
  • Cycling is additive cardio and does not replace leg day or the planned hamstring exposures.

Weekly review

Progression checkpoints

  • Track the next-day left-hamstring response to Flex B, especially single-leg RDLs.
  • Track 90/90 lift-off quality and the left hip response to internal-rotation work.
  • Track morning-after elbow stiffness following planche leans and pressing.
  • After approximately Aug 9, progress the left hamstring toward 7–9/10 contractions only if pain remains 0–2/10 with no next-day spike.
  • After Aug 16, consider left-forward half-split negatives and gradual stride reintroduction if symptom-stable.
  • Strength progression requires crisp sets and a quiet next day: add 1–2 reps or the smallest useful load increase, never both at once.
